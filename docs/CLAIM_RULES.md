# Claim rules (what the paper may say)

Gains are paired percentages over calibrated engines (positive = better). Material threshold: 0.5 percent. Bootstrap 95% CI of the median (2000 resamples).

| Level | Condition |
|---|---|
| CLAIM | lower CI bound > 0.5% AND at least 95% of engines improve AND design feasible on at least 90% of engines AND same sign under timing A and B (if both available) |
| CONDITIONAL | lower CI bound > 0 AND at least 80% of engines improve AND feasible on at least 90% (state the condition: e.g. only for RON 92) |
| INDICATIVE | median > 0 AND at least 60% improve |
| NOT SUPPORTED | anything else, or fewer than 30 engines, or feasible on less than 90% |

Words: only CLAIM may be written with "improves", "reduces", "increases". CONDITIONAL: "improves under condition X". INDICATIVE: "may". NOT SUPPORTED: "no evidence".
Every result is a model prediction conditional on calibration to published numbers; no real engine was tested.
