2. Read data/processed/cal_summary_B.json (and cal_summary_A.json if present).
3. Write reports/calibration_report.md containing (copy numbers from the json files):
 - the word `acceptance` and the acceptance rate for each timing reading,
 - how many engines were accepted,
 - the 5 calibration parameters whose `posterior_shrink` is smallest (they are the ones the published numbers pin down) and the 3 largest,
 - the accepted fraction when the power target is 4.5 hp instead of 5.05 (`acceptance_rate_power45`),
 - one sentence on whether timing A or B is easier to reconcile with the published numbers,
 - the accepted values of crank_rpm, leak_area_mm2, speedo_overread and cda_m2 (quartiles in cal_summary), and the number of engines dropped by the validity check (`dropped_invalid_baseline`, `dropped_reasons`),
 - one sentence saying whether the 80-90 km/h top speed target and the 12.7 bar cranking number could be met together.
